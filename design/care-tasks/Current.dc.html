Everything a task knows, on every row
The page shows authoring detail (recurrence, streak) while the keeper is trying to do a chore.
1
Two filters that disagree
The row labelled All Pets filters enclosures. The dropdown below labelled All Animals filters animals. Three filter rows, two of which overlap.
2
The same name, twice
Enclosure name and animal chip render side by side. When a keeper names the tank after the animal, the row says “Sir Rand Barnaby” twice.
3
The date the header already gave you
Under “Evening”, every row repeats “9/9 6:00 PM”. Under “Tomorrow”, “9/10 8:00 PM”. Inside a dated group the time alone is enough.
4
Three grouping axes in one scroll
Sections run morning → evening (time of day), then Tomorrow (relative day), then This Week (range). Three different ways of slicing time, stacked.
5
“All Done” is one quiet tap
It completes every task in the section with no confirm and no undo — writing feeding history the health engine later reads as fact.
6
Edit competes with done
Three near-identical buttons per row. Completing is almost everything anyone does here; editing a recurrence rule is rare.
7
No sense of finishing
Nothing counts what is left. A care routine is a thing you complete, and the page never says you did.
